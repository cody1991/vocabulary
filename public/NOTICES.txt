CET-6 flashcards — third-party data

public/cet6.json is a trimmed derivative:

- word, phonetic, defZh, and sentence come from KyleBing/english-vocabulary
  (CET-6 sentence JSONL: full_line_jsonl/sentence/正序/六级.jsonl).
- defEn is copied from the definition field of skywind3000/ECDICT.
  When ECDICT has several senses, the prep script keeps the first sense whose
  part of speech matches the Chinese gloss. It does not write new definitions.
  Headwords with an empty ECDICT definition have an empty defEn.

Audio is not stored here. The page requests a US pronunciation from Youdao
when you press Play audio:
https://dict.youdao.com/dictvoice?audio=WORD&type=2
